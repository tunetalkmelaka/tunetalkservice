Android & iPhone Mobile Internet Setup

If your TuneTalk mobile data is not working, check your APN settings. Use the TuneTalk APN configuration below and restart your mobile data after saving the settings.

TuneTalk APN Configuration

Name: tunetalk
APN: tunetalk
Username: Leave blank
Password: Leave blank
Other settings: Leave as default
After saving the APN, select tunetalk as the active APN and restart your mobile data. If necessary, restart your phone.

TuneTalk APN Settings for Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Mobile Network / SIM & Mobile Network.
  3. Select your TuneTalk SIM.
  4. Open Access Point Names (APN).
  5. Select New APN or the + button.
  6. Enter tunetalk for Name.
  7. Enter tunetalk for APN.
  8. Leave other fields unchanged or blank.
  9. Save the APN.
  10. Select the new TuneTalk APN and turn mobile data on again.

Menu names may be slightly different on Samsung, OPPO, vivo, HONOR, Xiaomi, Infinix, TECNO and other Android phones.

TuneTalk APN Settings for iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Mobile Service / Cellular.
  3. Select Mobile Data Network if available.
  4. Enter tunetalk in the APN field.
  5. Leave Username and Password blank.
  6. Return to the previous screen.
  7. Turn mobile data off and on again.

Depending on your iPhone configuration, the APN menu may not appear or may be configured automatically.
