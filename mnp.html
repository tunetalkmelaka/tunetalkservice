TuneTalk MNP Malaysia

Switch to TuneTalk & Keep Your Existing Mobile Number

Want to switch your current mobile number to TuneTalk? Mobile Number Portability (MNP) allows you to change your mobile service provider while keeping your existing Malaysian mobile number.

How to Switch to TuneTalk

Step 1:
Make sure your existing mobile number is active.
Step 2:
Submit your TuneTalk MNP / port-in registration.
Step 3:
Your current telco may send an SMS asking you to confirm the port-out request.
Step 4:
Reply to the confirmation SMS according to the instructions from your current telco.
Step 5:
Wait for the MNP process to be completed before using your TuneTalk SIM with your existing number.

Before Requesting MNP

Before switching to TuneTalk, check that:

✓ Your mobile number is still active.

✓ Your registration information matches your current telco records.

✓ You respond to any MNP confirmation SMS within the required time.

✓ Any applicable outstanding matters with your current provider are resolved.

Important:
Do not terminate your existing mobile number yourself before the MNP process is completed. The number normally needs to remain active for the port-in request.

Tukar Telco ke TuneTalk

Anda boleh bertukar ke TuneTalk melalui proses MNP sambil mengekalkan nombor telefon sedia ada. Pastikan nombor masih aktif dan ikuti SMS pengesahan daripada telco semasa anda.

携带原有号码转入 TuneTalk

通过 MNP（携号转网），您可以转入 TuneTalk，同时保留目前使用的手机号码。 请确保原有号码仍然有效，并按照原电讯公司的 SMS 指示确认转台申请。
